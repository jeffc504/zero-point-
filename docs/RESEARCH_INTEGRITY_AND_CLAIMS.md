# Research Integrity and Claims

This release distinguishes among four categories:

**Established physics:** phenomena such as the Casimir effect and experimentally observed dynamical-Casimir effects.

**Engineering proposals:** proposed toroidal cavities, graphene interfaces, sensor arrangements, vacuum systems, controls, and experimental packaging.

**Models/simulations:** numerical or analytical predictions that have not been experimentally verified in the proposed apparatus.

**Speculative hypotheses:** claims that a particular architecture might yield useful anomalous or net energy.

No simulation result should be presented as a measurement. No proposed enhancement factor should be presented as experimentally established without data. No output should be called vacuum-derived net energy until all supplied energy and measurement uncertainty have been independently accounted for.

The fastest route to credibility is not stronger language. It is better experiments.
