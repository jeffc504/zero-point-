# Experimental Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Documentation and model audit
Reproduce every equation and simulation from first principles. Identify assumptions, unsupported enhancement factors, numerical errors, and parameters requiring measurement.

## Phase 1 — Conventional benchmark
Reproduce established electromagnetic, cavity, and—where feasible—Casimir measurements before testing unusual configurations.

## Phase 2 — CORE ZERO-1 subsystem characterization
Test excitation hardware, toroidal structure, sensors, capacitor paths, control electronics, shielding, and thermal behavior separately. No anomalous-energy claim should be evaluated until conventional coupling paths are quantified.

## Phase 3 — Vacuum and environmental testing
Measure pressure-dependent behavior, thermal drift, vibration coupling, leakage, EMI/RFI coupling, grounding effects, and sensor offsets.

## Phase 4 — Casimir / dynamical-Casimir test platform
Use calibrated cavity structures and boundary modulation. Detect signals with redundant sensors and reference channels.

## Phase 5 — Graphene comparison
Run matched devices with and without graphene/graphene-hBN interfaces. Pre-register the comparison metric.

## Phase 6 — Closed energy audit
Instrument every power source: excitation, actuators, pumps, cryogenics, control electronics, bias supplies, detectors, cooling, and auxiliary systems. Measure output with independent instruments.

## Phase 7 — Adversarial controls
Run detuned structures, dummy loads, sham modulation, blinded configuration labels, EMI injection, vibration injection, thermal-equivalent controls, and detector-dark tests.

## Phase 8 — Independent replication
Provide another laboratory with the sanitized geometry, protocol, calibration procedure, raw data format, and analysis method.

A result should not be called net energy until an independent team reproduces a statistically and metrologically defensible excess after all inputs and uncertainty are included.
