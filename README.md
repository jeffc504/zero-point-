**Open conceptual research for rigorous testing, criticism, and independent replication.**

This repository shares a sanitized public release concerning two proposed research directions:

1. **VORTEX CORE ZERO-1** — a toroidal experimental platform.

> **Reality check:** These are research proposals—not a demonstrated source of usable “free energy.” No claim of net-positive energy has been experimentally validated here.

## What this project needs

Useful contributions include:

- identifying relevant peer-reviewed literature and prior art;
- improving measurement controls and uncertainty budgets;
- modeling ordinary electromagnetic, thermal, vibrational, and mechanical effects;
- proposing falsifiable test protocols;
- independently reproducing experiments and publishing null results;
- reviewing safety, instrumentation, and data-recording methods.

Please use GitHub Issues for technical questions, corrections, proposed experiments, and replication notes. Keep claims tied to measurements and cite sources.

## Scientific status

This release contains conceptual descriptions and proposed experimental methods. It does **not** contain measured net-energy results, runnable simulation code, raw experimental data, or complete fabrication drawings. References to simulations or earlier designs describe prior work; those files are not bundled here.

Any future result must account for all external energy inputs, calibration, thermal drift, electromagnetic coupling, vibration, stored energy, measurement uncertainty, and reproducibility. Extraordinary claims need strong, independently repeatable evidence. Null results and corrections are valuable and welcome.
